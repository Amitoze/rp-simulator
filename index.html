Retinitis pigmentosa,
as I see it

An artistic representation of one person's experience of losing sight.

Retinitis pigmentosa is an inherited eye condition in which the light-sensing cells of the retina slowly die, usually from the edges of vision inward. Clinical descriptions speak of tunnel vision and night blindness. What they rarely describe is what the lost field actually looks like from the inside. This is my attempt at capturing what I actually experience.

Caveats

RP varies enormously between people, in pattern, pace and texture; a different person would draw a different picture. This one is a portrait, not an average — an artistic interpretation of one experience, made to be felt rather than measured.

Photosensitivity warning

The simulation contains continuous rapid flashing at around 9 Hz. If you have photosensitive epilepsy or are sensitive to flickering light, please do not continue.
