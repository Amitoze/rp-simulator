## Community norms — what they say about builders recruiting

- **r/Blind / OurBlind**: Rule 2 — no feedback requests, surveys, product promotion, or DMs without mod approval; **surveys need a university ethics approval and supervising contact**; non-approved surveys banned outright; approval 1–2 business days; community explicitly wary of "survey saturation". [verified-fetch]
- **Facebook RP groups**: closed/admin-approved; assume "ask admins first". [uncertain]
- **ASSETS**: lived-experience authors welcomed via Experience Reports. **AATC / Vision 2027**: lived experience *wanted on the program*, not just in the audience. [verified-fetch]
- **PWDA ("nothing about us without us")**: proper remuneration for research participation; disabled people involved from outset to publication; authorship/IP recognition; won't review proposals sent within a month of a deadline; pwd@pwd.org.au. https://pwd.org.au/services/research-services/ [verified-fetch]
- **Payment norms**: ~¾ of Australian researchers pay ad hoc [uncertain]; practical baseline — pay in vouchers/cash, reimburse travel/support-worker costs. Leading with "I have RP and I built this" is the norm-unlocker in every one of these communities. [my-synthesis]
