## University-affiliated routes for an unaffiliated person

1. **PhD with stipend** — RTP base 2026 ~$34,315 tax-free (UniMelb tops up to ~$39,500) [uncertain]; Monash MATS explicitly recruits candidates with disability. Part-time PhD = half stipend.
2. **Consumer co-investigator payments** — Health Consumers NSW July 2025 guideline: **$43.50–$64.50/hr**, ~**$430/day** advisory; co-researchers explicitly in scope. Budget yourself at these rates into any MRFF / Retina Australia / MDI application. [verified-fetch] https://hcnsw.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Remuneration-and-reimbursement-of-health-consumers-FINAL-July-2025.pdf
3. **Honorary/adjunct + casual RA** — negotiated with a lab head (Marriott at MATS; CERA via Consumer Program). The simulator is the calling card.
4. **CERA Consumer Program** — unpaid entry, direct route to the IRD researchers who can host #4, #9 and Retina Australia bids.
